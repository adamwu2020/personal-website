About Me

I am a seasoned AI leader with over 15 years of experience in data science, machine learning, and applied AI. Currently, I serve as Director of AI at GE Healthcare, where I lead initiatives in the research and development of 3D medical foundation models. These models are trained and evaluated on real-world 2D and 3D medical images and clinical reports, enabling advanced capabilities in medical imaging analysis and diagnostics.

Previously, as an Applied Science Leader at Amazon, I led large language model (LLM) programs focused on prompt engineering, few-shot learning, and fine-tuning for a range of enterprise use cases. Throughout my career, I have consistently driven innovation at the intersection of AI research and practical application, delivering scalable solutions that create measurable business impact.

I hold a Ph.D. in Electronic Engineering and Information Science from the University of Science and Technology of China. My work spans leadership roles at top technology companies including GE, Amazon, Ancestry, Ultimate Software, and WeWork, where I applied expertise in generative AI, search algorithms, and algorithm design. I have authored publications in top-tier journals, filed multiple patents, and completed executive education at the UC Berkeley Haas School of Business and through Coursera.

I am passionate about applying data and AI to build transformative technologies that advance both business and society.
